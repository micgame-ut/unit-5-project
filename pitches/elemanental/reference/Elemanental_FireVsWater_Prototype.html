Parrying a light shot works, but you over-parry and your guard drops for a moment. Shooting is free; parry, dash and rush cost stamina. At zero you're winded.

Kits in this build

Fire

  • Light shots deal no damage. They build Burn (orange on the health bar), less through a shield.
  • Heavy shot and rush detonate Burn. A full bar triggers Flashpoint: big knockback.
  • Dash leaves a flame trail that burns and damages.

Water

  • Largest parry window. Parried heavy shots redirect with extra knockback.
  • Chain dash: the second dash leaves slick ground. Sliding players take more knockback.
  • Ebb: every 30 seconds, dash out of hitstun or a whiffed parry.

Not in this build: Earth, Air, map choice between rounds, 2v2.
